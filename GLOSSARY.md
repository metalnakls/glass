# Glass destinations

A download belongs to one destination, regardless of how Glass connects to it.

## Language

**Destination**:
The place that owns downloaded files: a folder on this Mac or a remote share.

**Share**:
A remote destination managed by Transmission on another machine. Its chosen share name identifies the destination; the server name is the fallback when no share is mapped.

**Server**:
The connection to a remote Transmission instance. Its configured download folder determines where new files arrive on that share.

**Recent folder**:
A previously used download folder on this Mac, available when choosing a local destination.
